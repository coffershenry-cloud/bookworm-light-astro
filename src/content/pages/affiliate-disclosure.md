# Affiliate Disclosure

InkGlobe is committed to providing useful, independent and transparent information to its readers. This page explains how affiliate links and other forms of commercial relationships may support this website.

## What Are Affiliate Links?

Some links published on InkGlobe are affiliate links. This means we may receive a commission, referral fee or other compensation when a visitor clicks a link or completes a qualifying action through that link.

Depending on the program, a qualifying action may include purchasing a product, requesting a quote, registering for a service or signing up for an account.

## Does It Cost You Anything?

Using an affiliate link generally does not increase the price you pay. The compensation, where applicable, is paid by the company or affiliate network rather than added to your purchase.

## How Affiliate Links Affect Our Content

Affiliate relationships do not automatically determine which companies, products or services we discuss.

Our aim is to publish content that is useful, relevant and based on research. We seek to explain the features, costs, limitations and considerations readers should understand before making a decision.

However, readers should understand that InkGlobe may receive compensation from some companies mentioned or linked to on the website.

## Third-Party Products and Services

InkGlobe may link to websites operated by other companies. We do not control those websites, their products, their services or their privacy practices.

Before purchasing a product, requesting a quote or sharing personal information with another company, readers should review that company's terms, policies and disclosures.

## Advertising and Sponsored Content

InkGlobe may display advertisements or publish sponsored material as the website develops. Where content is sponsored or an advertising relationship needs to be disclosed, we aim to identify that relationship clearly.

## Our Commitment to Readers

We aim to make commercial relationships transparent while continuing to publish useful information across finance, insurance and software.

Readers should make their own assessments before purchasing a product, choosing a provider or entering into a financial or insurance agreement.
