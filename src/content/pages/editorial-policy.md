# Editorial Policy

InkGlobe publishes educational and research-based content covering finance, insurance and software. Our editorial goal is to help readers understand products, services, costs, requirements and decisions that may affect individuals and businesses.

This policy explains the principles that guide how we prepare and maintain our content.

## Our Coverage

Our content focuses on three main areas:

- **Finance:** Business financing, credit products, loans, payment solutions and related financial topics.
- **Insurance:** Business and personal insurance, coverage options, costs, requirements and risk management.
- **Software:** Business software, digital tools, platforms and technology used by individuals and organizations.

## Research and Content Development

We aim to prepare articles using relevant information from available sources, including official company websites, product documentation, published pricing information, regulatory materials and other credible references.

The research used for an article depends on its subject. We aim to distinguish verifiable information from general explanations, opinions and practical considerations.

## Accuracy and Updates

We make reasonable efforts to ensure that published information is accurate when an article is prepared.

Some information changes over time. Product prices, insurance requirements, financial terms, software features and company policies may change without notice.

We may review and update articles when information becomes outdated or when meaningful corrections are necessary.

Readers should verify important details directly with the relevant provider before making a decision.

## Reviews and Comparisons

When comparing products, services or providers, we aim to consider relevant factors such as:

- Features and available services
- Pricing and potential costs
- Eligibility and requirements
- Benefits and limitations
- Intended users and business needs
- Important terms and conditions

The factors considered will vary depending on the subject. A product or provider mentioned in an article should not automatically be understood as suitable for every reader.

## Commercial Relationships

InkGlobe may participate in affiliate programs and other commercial partnerships. We may receive compensation when readers use certain links or complete qualifying actions.

Commercial relationships do not remove our commitment to providing useful and transparent information. Details about affiliate relationships are explained in our Affiliate Disclosure.

## Financial and Insurance Information

Articles about finance and insurance are provided for general informational and educational purposes.

They are not a substitute for professional financial, legal, tax or insurance advice. Individual circumstances differ, and readers should consult an appropriately qualified professional or provider when making decisions that require personal assessment.

## Corrections

If we identify an error in published content, we may correct or update the affected information.

Readers who notice a factual error or outdated detail can contact us through the Contact page.

## Editorial Independence

Our objective is to make content useful to readers. We aim to explain relevant information, including limitations and considerations, rather than presenting every product or service as the right choice.

## Contact

For editorial questions, corrections or feedback, please use the Contact page on InkGlobe.
