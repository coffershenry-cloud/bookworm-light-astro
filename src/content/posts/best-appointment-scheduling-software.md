  <h1 style="color: #172554; font-size: 34px; font-weight: 700; line-height: 1.25; margin: 0px 0px 20px;">Best Appointment Scheduling Software for Small Businesses in 2027</h1>

  <p style="margin: 0px 0px 18px;">Managing appointments can become surprisingly time-consuming when customers have to call, send messages or wait for someone to confirm an available time. A business owner may spend several hours each week answering booking questions, checking calendars, rescheduling customers and sending reminders.</p><p style="margin: 0px 0px 18px; text-align: center;"><img alt="Achieve small business customer service success. - AnswerConnect Blog" class="sFlh5c FyHeAf uT7N3d" height="427" src="https://storage.googleapis.com/answerconnect-blog/1/2023/08/image-7.png" style="--xgflle: 1.4992503748125936; max-width: 1000px;" width="640" />&nbsp;</p>

Some platforms even connect appointments with payment processing, customer records and point-of-sale systems.</p>

  <p style="margin: 0px 0px 24px;">However, not every scheduling tool is designed for the same purpose. A beauty salon accepting customer appointments has different needs from a consultant scheduling online meetings or an agency coordinating calls with prospective clients.</p>

  <h3 style="color: #1d4ed8; font-size: 23px; margin: 28px 0px 12px;">1. Setmore – Best for budget-conscious small teams</h3>

  <p style="margin: 0px 0px 16px;">Setmore is an online appointment scheduling platform designed to help service businesses manage bookings without relying entirely on phone calls and manual calendar updates.</p>

  <p style="margin: 0px 0px 16px;">Businesses can create a public booking page where customers select an available service, choose a time and submit their appointment details. The booking page can also be shared through a website or social media profile, making it easier for potential customers to schedule appointments independently.</p>

  <p style="margin: 0px 0px 16px;">One of Setmore's biggest advantages is its free plan. Unlike some competitors that restrict free accounts to one person, Setmore's website advertises support for up to four users on its free tier. This makes it attractive to small service businesses that want to coordinate several staff calendars without immediately paying for a subscription.</p>

  <h4 style="color: #172554; font-size: 18px; margin: 20px 0px 8px;">Key features</h4>
  <ul style="margin: 0px 0px 18px; padding-left: 22px;">
    <li>Online booking page for customer self-scheduling.</li>
    <li>Staff profiles and appointment calendars.</li>
    <li>Email confirmations and appointment reminders.</li>
    <li>Online payment options through supported integrations.</li>
    <li>Website and social media booking integrations.</li>
    <li>Mobile applications for managing appointments.</li>
    <li>Two-way calendar synchronization and SMS reminders on eligible paid plans.</li>
    <li>Recurring appointments and additional team features on Pro.</li>
  </ul>

  <h4 style="color: #172554; font-size: 18px; margin: 20px 0px 8px;">Pricing</h4>
  <p style="margin: 0px 0px 16px;">Setmore offers a free plan at <b>$0 per month</b> for up to four users. Its Pro plan costs <b>$5 per user per month when billed annually</b>, or $12 per user per month with monthly billing.</p>

  <p style="margin: 0px 0px 16px;">The free plan includes online booking, unlimited appointments, email reminders and several integrations. Pro adds features such as SMS reminders, two-way calendar synchronization, recurring appointments and removal of Setmore branding.</p>

  <h4 style="color: #172554; font-size: 18px; margin: 20px 0px 8px;">Pros</h4>
  <ul style="margin: 0px 0px 14px; padding-left: 22px;">

  <h2 style="color: #172554; font-size: 27px; margin: 38px 0px 16px;">How to choose the right appointment scheduling software</h2>

  <p style="margin: 0px 0px 18px;">The best scheduling platform depends on the services you offer, the number of people involved in delivering them and how customers are expected to book. Before subscribing, consider the following factors.</p>

  <h3 style="color: #1d4ed8; font-size: 20px; margin: 24px 0px 10px;">1. Understand the type of appointments you manage</h3>

  <p style="margin: 0px 0px 16px;">Start by identifying what customers are booking. A salon may need different appointment durations, employee schedules and deposits. A consultant may simply need a link that lets clients book a 30-minute meeting.</p>

  <p style="margin: 0px 0px 18px;">If you provide classes, workshops or group services, check whether the software supports group bookings and capacity limits. A meeting scheduler designed for one-to-one calls may not offer the same service management tools as a dedicated appointment platform.</p>

  <h3 style="color: #1d4ed8; font-size: 20px; margin: 24px 0px 10px;">2. Check the number of staff members and locations</h3>

  <p style="margin: 0px 0px 16px;">Some businesses have one person managing every appointment, while others have several employees providing different services. Your scheduling software should allow customers to book the correct person and should prevent overlapping appointments.</p>

  <p style="margin: 0px 0px 18px;">Also consider whether you operate from multiple locations. Check whether the provider charges per user, per seat, per location or according to the number of service providers included in the plan.</p>

  <h3 style="color: #1d4ed8; font-size: 20px; margin: 24px 0px 10px;">3. Look for payment and deposit features</h3>

  <p style="margin: 0px 0px 16px;">If customers pay for services when booking, you may need payment processing or deposit collection. These features can be particularly useful for businesses that reserve valuable time for each customer.</p>

  <p style="margin: 0px 0px 18px;">Check which payment processors are supported, whether the feature requires a paid subscription and what transaction fees apply. A booking platform's subscription price does not necessarily include payment processing costs.</p>

  <h3 style="color: #1d4ed8; font-size: 20px; margin: 24px 0px 10px;">4. Compare reminders and cancellation management</h3>

  <p style="margin: 0px 0px 16px;">Appointment reminders help customers remember their bookings and give them an opportunity to reschedule when necessary. Some platforms include email reminders in their free plans, while SMS reminders may require an upgrade or additional credits.</p>

  <p style="margin: 0px 0px 18px;">Look for cancellation policies, rescheduling controls, waitlists and no-show fee options if missed appointments create significant losses for your business.</p>

  <h3 style="color: #1d4ed8; font-size: 20px; margin: 24px 0px 10px;">5. Consider integrations with your existing software</h3>

  <p style="margin: 0px 0px 16px;">Your scheduling system should work with the tools your business already uses. These may include Google Calendar, Microsoft Outlook, Zoom, accounting software, customer relationship management platforms and payment processors.</p>

  <p style="margin: 0px 0px 18px;">For example, a sales team using Zoho CRM may benefit from Zoho Bookings, while a business already accepting payments through Square may prefer Square Appointments.</p>

  <h3 style="color: #1d4ed8; font-size: 20px; margin: 24px 0px 10px;">6. Calculate the total cost as your business grows</h3>

  <p style="margin: 0px 0px 16px;">A free scheduling plan can be attractive at first, but the cost may increase when you add more employees, locations, booking types or monthly appointments.</p>

  <p style="margin: 0px 0px 18px;">Compare the cost of the plan your business will realistically need, not just the cheapest advertised subscription. Include optional SMS credits, payment processing, additional workspaces and integrations in your calculation.</p>

    <h3 style="color: #172554; font-size: 18px; margin: 0px 0px 9px;">What is the best appointment scheduling software for a small business?</h3>

    <h3 style="color: #172554; font-size: 18px; margin: 0px 0px 9px;">Is there free appointment scheduling software for small businesses?</h3>
    <p style="margin: 0px;">Yes. Setmore, Square Appointments, SimplyBook.me, Zoho Bookings and Calendly offer free plans with different limits.     <p style="margin: 0px;">Many appointment scheduling platforms support online payments through connected payment processors. Square Appointments, Acuity and eligible SimplyBook.me and Zoho Bookings plans offer payment-related features. Calendly also supports payment integrations on paid plans.   <div style="border-radius: 7px; border: 1px solid rgb(219, 227, 239); margin: 14px 0px; padding: 18px 20px;">
    <h3 style="color: #172554; font-size: 18px; margin: 0px 0px 9px;">What is the difference between appointment scheduling software and calendar software?</h3>
    <p style="margin: 0px;">Calendar software primarily helps individuals and teams organize their schedules. Appointment scheduling software adds customer-facing booking pages, available time slots, confirmations, reminders and booking rules. Some platforms combine both functions, allowing appointments to synchronize with existing calendars.</p>
  </div>

  <div style="border-radius: 7px; border: 1px solid rgb(219, 227, 239); margin: 14px 0px; padding: 18px 20px;">
    <h3 style="color: #172554; font-size: 18px; margin: 0px 0px 9px;">Does appointment scheduling software help reduce no-shows?</h3>
    <p style="margin: 0px;">It can help by sending appointment confirmations and reminders before scheduled visits. Some platforms also support deposits, prepayments, cancellation policies and no-show fees. These features can make it easier for businesses to manage cancellations, although they cannot guarantee that every customer will attend.</p>
  </div>

  <div style="border-radius: 7px; border: 1px solid rgb(219, 227, 239); margin: 14px 0px; padding: 18px 20px;">
    <h3 style="color: #172554; font-size: 18px; margin: 0px 0px 9px;">Can customers book appointments without creating an account?</h3>
    <p style="margin: 0px;">Many booking platforms allow customers to select a time and submit their details through a public booking page without creating a separate software account. This makes the process easier for new customers. Check the individual platform's booking settings to understand which information customers must provide.</p>
  </div>
