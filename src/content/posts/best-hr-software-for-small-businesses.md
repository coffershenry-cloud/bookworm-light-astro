  <p style="color: #414650; font-size: 19px; line-height: 1.8; margin-bottom: 22px;">
    Managing employees involves much more than paying salaries. Small business owners also need to organize employee records, handle onboarding paperwork, track time off, manage workplace policies and keep important employment documents accessible. As a team grows, handling all these responsibilities through spreadsheets, emails and paper files can become difficult.&nbsp;</p><p style="color: #414650; font-size: 19px; line-height: 1.8; margin-bottom: 22px;"><img alt="Records Management &amp; Storage for Small Businesses | Corodata" class="sFlh5c Jm0Uqe FyHeAf uT7N3d" height="436" src="https://corodata.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/Small-Business-Owner-1024x698.jpg" style="--xgflle: 1.4670487106017192; max-width: 1024px;" width="640" />&nbsp;</p>

  <p>
    Human resources (HR) software helps businesses organize these tasks in one place. Depending on the platform, it can support employee records, recruitment, onboarding, attendance, performance reviews, benefits administration and payroll coordination.
  </p>

  <p>
    However, not every small business needs a complicated human resources system. A company with five employees may simply need an affordable employee database and leave tracker. A business with 30 employees may need onboarding workflows, performance management and reporting. A company with hourly workers may care more about scheduling and attendance.
  </p>

  <p>
    In this guide, we compare five HR software options for small businesses in 2026: BambooHR, Gusto, Zoho People, Rippling and Homebase. We examine their features, published pricing, advantages and limitations to help you choose a platform that fits your workforce.
  </p>

    </p>

  <h2 id="bamboohr" style="border-bottom: 2px solid rgb(232, 234, 240); color: #253858; font-size: 27px; margin-top: 40px; padding-bottom: 10px;">1. BambooHR: Best for Dedicated HR Management</h2>

  <p>
    <b>BambooHR</b> is a human resources information system (HRIS) designed to help organizations manage employee information and everyday HR processes. It gives businesses a centralized place to organize employee records, manage hiring, handle onboarding and track important workforce information.&nbsp;</p><p><img alt="BambooHR Logo, symbol, meaning, history, PNG, brand" class="sFlh5c Jm0Uqe FyHeAf uT7N3d" height="360" src="https://logos-world.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/BambooHR-Logo.png" style="--xgflle: 1.7777777777777777; max-width: 3840px;" width="640" />&nbsp;</p>

  <p>
    Rather than focusing primarily on payroll, BambooHR puts more emphasis on the employee management side of running a business. This makes it worth considering for companies that have outgrown spreadsheets and need a more organized HR system.
  </p>

  <div style="background: rgb(245, 247, 251); border-radius: 8px; border: 1px solid rgb(224, 229, 238); margin: 20px 0px; padding: 18px 20px;">
    <h3 style="color: #253858; font-size: 18px; margin: 0px 0px 12px;">BambooHR Pricing and Features</h3>
    <ul style="margin: 0px; padding-left: 22px;">
      <li><b>Core:</b> $10.25 per employee per month for companies with more than 25 employees, or $250 per month for companies with 25 employees or fewer.</li>
      <li><b>Pro:</b> $17.50 per employee per month for companies with more than 25 employees, or $425 per month for companies with 25 employees or fewer.</li>
      <li><b>Elite:</b> $25 per employee per month for companies with more than 25 employees, or $650 per month for companies with 25 employees or fewer.</li>
      <li><b>Core features:</b> Employee records, reporting, workflows, approvals, hiring and onboarding tools.</li>
      <li><b>Pro features:</b> Additional performance management, employee experience and recognition tools.</li>
      <li><b>Elite features:</b> Advanced compensation management, analytics and HR benchmarks.</li>
    </ul>
  </div>

  <h3 style="color: #37445c; font-size: 19px;">What Makes BambooHR Worth Considering?</h3>

  <p>
    BambooHR is useful for businesses that need a proper HR system rather than a basic employee directory. Employee information can be stored in one organized database, making it easier to access records and manage important administrative processes.
  </p>

  <p>
    Hiring and onboarding tools can help businesses manage candidates and move new employees through the onboarding process. Workflows and approvals also reduce the need to handle every HR task through separate emails or spreadsheets.
  </p>

  <p>
    Higher plans provide additional tools for performance reviews, employee recognition and compensation planning. These features may become more useful as a business grows and needs more structured people management.
  </p>

  <p>
    BambooHR also offers additional capabilities such as payroll and benefits administration. These are important to consider separately when comparing the total cost of the platform.
  </p>

  <h3 style="color: #37445c; font-size: 19px;">What to Consider</h3>

  <p>
    BambooHR may be expensive for a very small business with only a handful of employees. Its Core plan has a flat monthly price of $250 for companies with 25 employees or fewer, so a five-person business should not assume that it will pay only $10.25 per employee.
  </p>

  <p>
    Payroll and certain other capabilities are additional products or add-ons. A business looking for payroll-first software may find Gusto's published pricing easier to understand.
  </p>

  <p>
    Some advanced features are reserved for higher plans. Compare the Core, Pro and Elite packages carefully rather than assuming that every BambooHR subscription includes the complete HR toolkit.
  </p>

  <h2 id="gusto" style="border-bottom: 2px solid rgb(232, 234, 240); color: #253858; font-size: 27px; margin-top: 40px; padding-bottom: 10px;">2. Gusto: Best for Payroll and HR Together</h2>

  <p>
    <b>Gusto</b> is a payroll and HR platform designed primarily for US businesses. It combines payroll processing with employee administration, benefits-related tools, onboarding and other workforce features.</p><p><img alt="Gusto, Inc. - Wikipedia" class="sFlh5c Jm0Uqe FyHeAf uT7N3d" height="427" src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/03/Gusto%2C_Inc._logo.svg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&amp;utm_campaign=index&amp;utm_content=original" style="--xgflle: 1.5003750937734435; max-width: 2000px;" width="640" />&nbsp;</p>

  <p>
    This makes it a practical option for small businesses that want to manage payroll and basic HR responsibilities through one platform instead of purchasing separate systems for every task.
  </p>

  <div style="background: rgb(245, 247, 251); border-radius: 8px; border: 1px solid rgb(224, 229, 238); margin: 20px 0px; padding: 18px 20px;">
    <h3 style="color: #253858; font-size: 18px; margin: 0px 0px 12px;">Gusto Pricing and Features</h3>
    <ul style="margin: 0px; padding-left: 22px;">
      <li><b>Simple:</b> $49 per month plus $6 per person per month.</li>
      <li><b>Plus:</b> $80 per month plus $12 per person per month.</li>
      <li><b>Premium:</b> $180 per month plus $22 per person per month.</li>
      <li><b>Simple features:</b> Unlimited payrolls, tax filings and payments, basic paid time off policies and holiday pay.</li>
      <li><b>Plus features:</b> Time tracking, scheduling, multi-state payroll and contractor invoice management.</li>
      <li><b>Premium features:</b> Premium account support, HR guidance and additional compliance tools.</li>
    </ul>
  </div>

  <h3 style="color: #37445c; font-size: 19px;">What Makes Gusto Worth Considering?</h3>

  <p>
    Gusto is particularly useful when payroll is one of the biggest administrative challenges facing a small business. The platform supports payroll processing, tax filings and payments, helping owners manage recurring payroll responsibilities.
  </p>

  <p>
    It also includes employee-facing tools. Depending on the plan, employees can access payroll information and other self-service features without having to ask the business owner for every document.
  </p>

  <p>
    Higher plans add workforce management capabilities such as time tracking, scheduling and multi-state payroll. Gusto also provides additional HR tools and access to HR guidance through eligible plans and services.
  </p>

  <p>
    For a small US business that wants payroll and HR-related administration connected, this can reduce the number of separate platforms needed.
  </p>

  <h3 style="color: #37445c; font-size: 19px;">What to Consider</h3>

  <p>
    Gusto is payroll-first software, not a dedicated HRIS with every advanced people-management feature. A business that needs extensive performance management, compensation planning or advanced employee analytics may prefer a more specialized HR platform.
  </p>

  <p>
    The per-person fee increases as the workforce grows. For example, a business with 10 employees on the Simple plan would pay $109 per month before any additional services or applicable charges.
  </p>

  <p>
    Gusto's payroll and tax services are designed around the US market. Businesses operating in other countries should check local payroll support and employment requirements rather than assuming that the US product handles their jurisdiction.
  </p>

  <p>
    <b>Zoho People</b> is an HR management platform that helps businesses organize employee information and everyday HR processes. It is particularly attractive to smaller teams because it offers a free plan and relatively low per-user pricing on its paid plans.&nbsp;</p><p><img alt="Zoho People Features | HR Software Features" class="sFlh5c Jm0Uqe FyHeAf uT7N3d" height="215" src="https://encrypted-tbn0.gstatic.com/images?q=tbn:ANd9GcRiOKsx4yc11hEZ6ppUJO10B4oAqWu44piorErIPc1T1c6m1vLqK1SY-M90&amp;s=10" style="--xgflle: 2.976833976833977; max-width: 800px;" width="640" />&nbsp;</p>

  <p>
    The software focuses on employee management rather than trying to serve as a complete payroll system. Businesses can use it to manage employee records, documents, leave, onboarding, attendance and other HR activities depending on their subscription.
  </p>

  <div style="background: rgb(245, 247, 251); border-radius: 8px; border: 1px solid rgb(224, 229, 238); margin: 20px 0px; padding: 18px 20px;">

  <h3 style="color: #37445c; font-size: 19px;">What Makes Zoho People Worth Considering?</h3>

  <p>
    Affordability is one of Zoho People's strongest advantages. A small business that only needs a basic employee database and leave management may be able to start with the free plan.
  </p>

  <p>
    The paid plans add features as the business becomes more organized. Essential HR includes onboarding and offboarding, while Professional adds attendance management, timesheets and roster management.
  </p>

  <p>
    Premium introduces performance and compensation management, employee engagement and advanced HR analytics. Enterprise adds capabilities such as an HR help desk and learning management system.
  </p>

  <p>
    Zoho People can also fit into the wider Zoho software ecosystem. Businesses already using other Zoho applications may find it convenient to connect HR activities with their existing business tools.
  </p>

  <h3 style="color: #37445c; font-size: 19px;">What to Consider</h3>

  <p>

  </p>

  <p>
    Some features are only available on higher plans. A business should compare the exact plan requirements before choosing the cheapest option.
  </p>

  <p>
    The published annual prices are low, but the business should check the minimum-user requirement, billing frequency and local taxes before calculating its final subscription cost.
  </p>

  <h2 id="rippling" style="border-bottom: 2px solid rgb(232, 234, 240); color: #253858; font-size: 27px; margin-top: 40px; padding-bottom: 10px;">4. Rippling: Best for Connecting HR and IT Management</h2>

  <p>
    <b>Rippling</b> is a modular workforce management platform that connects HR with other business functions, including payroll and IT management. Instead of treating employee administration as a separate process, it helps businesses manage employee information and related workflows across different systems.&nbsp;</p><p><img alt="Rippling (company) - Wikipedia" class="sFlh5c Jm0Uqe FyHeAf uT7N3d" src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/32/Rippling-Logo.jpg?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&amp;utm_campaign=index&amp;utm_content=original" style="--xgflle: 2.0120967741935485; max-width: 499px;" />&nbsp;</p>

  <p>
    This can be useful for growing companies where onboarding a new employee involves more than collecting paperwork. The business may also need to create user accounts, assign software access, arrange equipment and coordinate payroll information.
  </p>

  <div style="background: rgb(245, 247, 251); border-radius: 8px; border: 1px solid rgb(224, 229, 238); margin: 20px 0px; padding: 18px 20px;">
    <h3 style="color: #253858; font-size: 18px; margin: 0px 0px 12px;">Rippling Pricing and Features</h3>
    <ul style="margin: 0px; padding-left: 22px;">
      <li><b>Starting price:</b> From $8 per employee per month, plus a monthly base fee.</li>
      <li><b>Pricing structure:</b> Modular, with a customized quote based on the products selected and workforce size.</li>
      <li><b>HR features:</b> Employee information, onboarding workflows and workforce administration.</li>
      <li><b>Payroll:</b> Available as part of the wider platform, with pricing based on selected products and services.</li>
      <li><b>IT management:</b> Options for managing employee access, applications and devices.</li>
      <li><b>Integrations and automation:</b> Connects different business systems and supports workflows across products.</li>
    </ul>
  </div>

  <h3 style="color: #37445c; font-size: 19px;">What Makes Rippling Worth Considering?</h3>

  <p>
    Rippling's main advantage is its ability to connect HR with other business operations. When a new employee joins, a business may need to update several systems at once. A connected platform can reduce repeated administrative work.
  </p>

  <p>
    Businesses can select the products they need instead of automatically purchasing every available module. This modular structure can be useful for companies that want to expand their HR and IT systems gradually.
  </p>

  <p>

  <h2 id="what-is" style="border-bottom: 2px solid rgb(232, 234, 240); color: #253858; font-size: 27px; margin-top: 40px; padding-bottom: 10px;">What Does HR Software Do for a Small Business?</h2>

  <p>
    HR software helps businesses manage information and processes related to employees. Instead of maintaining separate spreadsheets, documents and email conversations, a company can use a central platform to organize important HR activities.
  </p>

  <h3 style="color: #37445c; font-size: 20px;">1. Employee Records Management</h3>

  <p>
    Employee records management involves storing and organizing information about workers. This may include contact details, job titles, employment dates, documents, emergency contacts and other relevant information.
  </p>

  <p>
    A centralized employee database makes it easier for authorized managers to find and update information when necessary.
  </p>

  <h3 style="color: #37445c; font-size: 20px;">2. Recruitment and Onboarding</h3>

  <p>
    Some HR platforms help businesses manage job applicants, track recruitment stages and prepare new employees for their first day.
  </p>

  <p>
    Onboarding tools can organize forms, company policies, required documents and introductory tasks. This can make it easier to give new employees a consistent starting experience.
  </p>

  <h3 style="color: #37445c; font-size: 20px;">3. Leave and Attendance Management</h3>

  <p>
    HR software can help employees request time off and allow managers to review and approve those requests. Depending on the platform, it may also track attendance, working hours, timesheets and shift schedules.
  </p>

  <p>
    This is particularly useful for businesses with hourly workers or employees working different shifts.
  </p>

  <h3 style="color: #37445c; font-size: 20px;">4. Payroll and Benefits Administration</h3>

  <p>
    Some platforms combine HR with payroll processing and benefits administration. These tools may help businesses calculate employee pay, process deductions, prepare payroll information and manage benefits enrollment.
  </p>

  <p>
    Not every HR system includes payroll. Some platforms integrate with separate payroll applications, while others offer payroll as an additional product or paid module.
  </p>

  <h3 style="color: #37445c; font-size: 20px;">5. Performance Management</h3>

  <p>
    Performance management features help businesses organize employee goals, feedback, reviews and development activities.
  </p>

  <p>
    These tools can become more useful as a business grows and managers need a consistent way to evaluate performance and communicate expectations.
  </p>

  <h3 style="color: #37445c; font-size: 20px;">6. HR Reporting and Compliance Support</h3>

  <p>
    Some platforms provide reports that help businesses review workforce information, employee changes, attendance and other HR data.
  </p>

  <p>
    Certain systems also provide compliance-related reminders, document management and access to HR guidance. However, software does not automatically make a business legally compliant. Employers remain responsible for understanding and following the employment laws that apply to them.
  </p>

  <h2 id="features" style="border-bottom: 2px solid rgb(232, 234, 240); color: #253858; font-size: 27px; margin-top: 40px; padding-bottom: 10px;">Important Features to Look for in Small Business HR Software</h2>

  <p>
    Before choosing a platform, identify the HR tasks that take the most time in your business. A useful system should solve those problems without introducing unnecessary complexity.
  </p>

  <h3 style="color: #37445c; font-size: 20px;">1. A Centralized Employee Database</h3>

  <p>
    Look for a platform that allows authorized users to store and update employee records in one place. Check whether it supports document storage, employee profiles, role information and access permissions.
  </p>

  <h3 style="color: #37445c; font-size: 20px;">2. Onboarding and Offboarding Workflows</h3>

  <p>
    Onboarding features can help businesses prepare employment documents, assign tasks and organize new-hire information. Offboarding workflows can help managers handle departure procedures and recover company property.
  </p>

  <h3 style="color: #37445c; font-size: 20px;">3. Employee Self-Service</h3>

  <p>
    Employee self-service allows workers to access certain information and complete basic tasks themselves. Depending on the platform, employees may be able to update personal details, request leave, view documents or access pay information.
  </p>

  <p>
    This can reduce the number of routine questions directed to managers and administrative staff.
  </p>

  <h3 style="color: #37445c; font-size: 20px;">4. Payroll Integration</h3>

  <p>
    If payroll is a major priority, check whether the HR system includes payroll or connects with a payroll provider you already use.
  </p>

  <p>
    Confirm which countries and states are supported, how tax information is handled and whether payroll services cost extra.
  </p>

  <h3 style="color: #37445c; font-size: 20px;">5. Time Tracking and Leave Management</h3>

  <p>
    Businesses with hourly workers should look for attendance tracking, timesheets, shift scheduling and overtime information. Office-based teams may place more importance on leave requests and approval workflows.
  </p>

  <h3 style="color: #37445c; font-size: 20px;">6. Reporting and Analytics</h3>

  <p>
    Reporting tools can help managers review employee information, workforce changes and other administrative data. More advanced systems may also provide performance, compensation and workforce analytics.
  </p>

  <h3 style="color: #37445c; font-size: 20px;">7. Security and Access Controls</h3>

  <p>
    HR systems store sensitive employee information. Check whether the provider offers role-based permissions, secure authentication, data protection controls and clear information about how employee data is stored and processed.
  </p>

  <p>
    Give access only to people who need it for their work, and review permissions when employees change roles or leave the organization.
  </p>

  <h2 id="cost" style="border-bottom: 2px solid rgb(232, 234, 240); color: #253858; font-size: 27px; margin-top: 40px; padding-bottom: 10px;">How Much Does HR Software Cost for a Small Business?</h2>

  <p>
    HR software pricing depends on the platform, workforce size and features selected. Some providers charge per employee, others charge a monthly base fee plus a per-person amount, and some use a per-location pricing model.
  </p>

  <p>
    A free plan may be enough for a very small team, while a business that needs payroll, benefits and advanced HR workflows may have to budget for a more comprehensive subscription.
  </p>

  <div style="background: rgb(245, 247, 251); border-radius: 8px; border: 1px solid rgb(224, 229, 238); margin: 22px 0px; padding: 18px 20px;">
    <h3 style="color: #253858; font-size: 18px; margin: 0px 0px 12px;">Common HR Software Pricing Models</h3>
    <ul style="margin: 0px; padding-left: 22px;">
      <li><b>Free plans:</b> Limited features and restrictions on users, locations or functionality.</li>
      <li><b>Per-employee pricing:</b> The subscription increases with the number of employees.</li>
      <li><b>Base fee plus employee fee:</b> A fixed monthly charge is combined with a cost for each worker.</li>
      <li><b>Per-location pricing:</b> The business pays based on the number of workplaces using the software.</li>
      <li><b>Custom pricing:</b> The provider prepares a quote based on the modules, workforce size and services selected.</li>
    </ul>
  </div>

  <p>
    Do not compare software using only its advertised starting price. A low per-user fee may exclude payroll, benefits, advanced reporting or other services that your business needs.
  </p>

  <p>
    Before subscribing, calculate the total cost for your actual workforce and ask whether there are additional charges for implementation, support, integrations or optional modules.
  </p>

  <h2 id="faqs" style="border-bottom: 2px solid rgb(232, 234, 240); color: #253858; font-size: 27px; margin-top: 40px; padding-bottom: 10px;">Frequently Asked Questions About Small Business HR Software</h2>

  <h3 style="color: #37445c; font-size: 19px;">1. What is the best HR software for a small business?</h3>

  <p>
    The best option depends on the company's requirements. BambooHR is a strong choice for dedicated HR management, Gusto is useful for US businesses that want payroll and HR together, Zoho People is attractive for affordable employee management, Rippling connects HR with IT, and Homebase is suited to hourly teams.
  </p>

  <h3 style="color: #37445c; font-size: 19px;">2. Is there free HR software for small businesses?</h3>

  <p>
    Yes. Zoho People offers a free plan for up to five users, while Homebase offers a free Basic plan for one location and up to 10 employees. Free plans have feature and usage limitations, so check whether they cover your actual requirements.
  </p>

  <h3 style="color: #37445c; font-size: 19px;">3. What is the difference between HR software and payroll software?</h3>

  <p>
    HR software manages employee information and workforce processes such as onboarding, leave, attendance and performance. Payroll software focuses on calculating and processing employee payments, deductions and related tax responsibilities. Some platforms combine both functions.
  </p>

  <h3 style="color: #37445c; font-size: 19px;">4. Does a small business really need HR software?</h3>

  <p>
    Not every small business needs a paid HR platform. However, software can become useful when employee information, onboarding documents, leave requests and workforce administration become difficult to manage manually. A free or basic plan may be enough for a very small team.
  </p>

  <h3 style="color: #37445c; font-size: 19px;">5. How much does HR software cost per employee?</h3>

  <p>
    Pricing varies by provider. Zoho People publishes annual per-user plans starting at $1.25 per month, while Rippling publishes a starting price of $8 per employee per month plus a base fee. BambooHR uses different pricing for companies with 25 or fewer employees, and Gusto combines a monthly base fee with a per-person charge.
  </p>

  <h3 style="color: #37445c; font-size: 19px;">6. Can HR software handle employee onboarding?</h3>

  <p>
    Many HR platforms include onboarding features. These can help businesses organize new-hire information, documents, tasks and employee records. The exact capabilities depend on the provider and subscription plan.
  </p>

  <h3 style="color: #37445c; font-size: 19px;">7. Does HR software guarantee legal compliance?</h3>

  <p>
    No. HR software can help organize records, provide reminders and support certain administrative processes, but it does not guarantee legal compliance. Employers must still understand the employment laws and regulations that apply to their business and seek qualified professional advice when necessary.
  </p>

  <h3 style="color: #37445c; font-size: 19px;">8. Can HR software integrate with accounting and payroll applications?</h3>

  <p>
    Many platforms offer integrations with payroll, accounting, communication and other business applications. However, available integrations vary by provider and plan. Check the integration directory before purchasing if your business relies on a particular application.
  </p>
