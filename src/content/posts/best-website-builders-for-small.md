<h1 style="color: #172554; font-size: 34px; font-weight: 700; line-height: 1.25; margin: 0px 0px 18px;">Best Website Builders for Small Businesses in 2027</h1>

<p style="color: #475569; font-size: 18px; line-height: 1.7;">A professional website can help a small business attract customers, explain its services, showcase products and build credibility. But creating one does not necessarily require hiring a web developer or learning how to code. Website builders make it possible to design, publish and manage a business website using templates and visual editing tools.</p><p style="color: #475569; font-size: 18px; line-height: 1.7; text-align: center;"><img alt="Small Business Website Design Best Practices (2026) – Forbes Advisor" class="sFlh5c FyHeAf uT7N3d" height="361" src="https://thumbor.forbes.com/thumbor/fit-in/900x510/https://www.forbes.com/advisor/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/Image_-_Web_Design_.jpeg.jpg" style="--xgflle: 1.7751479289940828; max-width: 900px;" width="640" />&nbsp;</p><p>The challenge is choosing the right platform. Some website builders are designed for businesses that need a simple online presence, while others provide advanced ecommerce tools, appointment booking, marketing features and greater design flexibility. Their pricing structures also differ considerably.</p>

<p>A platform that looks inexpensive at first may require a more expensive plan to accept payments, connect a custom domain or access important features. Other providers advertise promotional prices that increase significantly when the initial subscription expires.</p>

<p>In this guide, we compare six website builders for small businesses in 2027: Wix, Squarespace, Hostinger, Shopify, WordPress.com and Webflow. We examine their features, pricing, advantages, limitations and the types of businesses each platform is best suited to.</p>

<p style="margin: 0px;">For businesses primarily selling products online, <b>Shopify</b> is a more specialised ecommerce platform. <b>WordPress.com</b> is worth considering for content-heavy websites and businesses that need plugins, while <b>Webflow</b> suits design-focused websites that require greater visual control.</p>

<h2 style="border-bottom-color: rgb(219, 234, 254); border-bottom-style: solid; border-bottom-width: 2px; border-bottom: 2px solid rgb(219, 234, 254); color: #172554; font-size: 27px; margin-top: 42px; padding-bottom: 10px;">1. Wix: Best Overall Website Builder for Small Businesses</h2>

<p><b>Best for:</b> Small businesses that want an easy-to-use website builder with design, marketing, booking and ecommerce capabilities.</p>

<p><b>Starting paid price:</b> Approximately $17 per month on the Light plan with annual billing.</p>

<p><b>Free plan:</b> Yes, with Wix branding and a Wix subdomain.</p>

<p>Wix is a popular website-building platform that combines a visual editor with a large collection of templates and business features. It allows users to create websites without writing code, making it accessible to entrepreneurs who want to establish an online presence without managing the technical side of website development.</p>

<p>Businesses can choose from templates, customise page layouts, add images and text, create contact forms and connect additional services through the platform's app ecosystem.</p>

<p>Wix is particularly useful for businesses that need more than a basic collection of web pages. Depending on the subscription, users can add appointment scheduling, accept payments, sell products and access marketing tools.</p>

<h3 style="color: #1d4ed8; font-size: 21px; margin-top: 25px;">Key Features</h3>

<ul style="padding-left: 24px;">
<li>Visual drag-and-drop website editor.</li>
<li>Large selection of customisable website templates.</li>
<li>AI-assisted website creation tools.</li>
<li>Built-in hosting and SSL security.</li>
<li>Business tools for bookings, services and online payments on eligible plans.</li>
<li>Marketing and SEO features.</li>
<li>App marketplace for additional functionality.</li>
</ul>

<h3 style="color: #1d4ed8; font-size: 21px; margin-top: 25px;">Wix Pricing</h3>

<p>Wix offers a free plan and several paid subscriptions. Its published plan structure includes:</p>

<ul style="padding-left: 24px;">
<li><b>Free:</b> $0, with Wix branding and a Wix-branded web address.</li>
<li><b>Light:</b> Approximately $17 per month with annual billing.</li>
<li><b>Core:</b> Approximately $29 per month with annual billing.</li>
<li><b>Business:</b> Approximately $39 per month with annual billing.</li>
<li><b>Business Elite:</b> Approximately $159 per month with annual billing.</li>
</ul>

<p>The Light plan is intended for a basic professional website. However, businesses that need to accept online payments or operate an online store should examine Core or higher plans.</p>

<p>Core includes basic ecommerce and payment capabilities, while higher subscriptions provide additional storage, collaborators and business features. The right plan depends on the functions the website needs rather than simply the number of pages it contains.</p>

<p>Wix states that prices can vary by location, and annual subscription prices are generally paid upfront. Confirm the final amount displayed at checkout before purchasing.</p>

<p><b>Advantages</b></p>
<ul style="padding-left: 24px;">
<li>Beginner-friendly visual website editor.</li>
<li>Large template collection.</li>
<li>Business and ecommerce tools available within the platform.</li>
<li>Hosting and website security included in its plans.</li>
<li>Suitable for different small-business website types.</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Disadvantages</b></p>
<ul style="padding-left: 24px;">
<li>The free plan is not suitable for a fully branded professional website.</li>
<li>Online payment and ecommerce capabilities require eligible paid plans.</li>
<li>Advanced features can increase the subscription cost.</li>
<li>Moving a Wix website to a different platform is not a straightforward full-site export process.</li>

<p><b>Our verdict:</b> Wix is a strong all-around option for small businesses that want design flexibility and a range of built-in business tools without managing separate hosting. It is especially worth considering for service businesses, consultants and local companies that need a professional website with room to add bookings or ecommerce later.</p>

<p>Squarespace is known for its professionally designed templates and visual presentation. It is particularly attractive to businesses where the appearance of the website plays an important role in attracting customers.</p>

<p>Photographers, designers, consultants, beauty professionals and creative agencies may appreciate its layouts for showcasing portfolios, services, customer testimonials and business information.</p>

<p>The platform also includes hosting, website editing tools, SEO functionality and selling features. Businesses can add products and services, accept payments and create a website that reflects their brand without building everything from scratch.</p>

<h3 style="color: #1d4ed8; font-size: 21px; margin-top: 25px;">Key Features</h3>

<li>Product and service selling capabilities.</li>

<h3 style="color: #1d4ed8; font-size: 21px; margin-top: 25px;">Squarespace Pricing</h3>

<p>Squarespace currently offers four main website subscriptions:</p>

<ul style="padding-left: 24px;">
<li><b>Basic:</b> $19 per month with annual billing, or $25 billed monthly.</li>
<li><b>Core:</b> $29 per month with annual billing, or $39 billed monthly.</li>
<li><b>Plus:</b> $49 per month with annual billing, or $65 billed monthly.</li>
<li><b>Advanced:</b> $99 per month with annual billing, or $139 billed monthly.</li>
</ul>

<p>The Basic plan provides the essentials for a professional website and includes a custom domain offer for eligible annual subscriptions. However, it carries a 2% transaction fee on certain sales of physical products, services and other eligible commerce transactions.</p>

<p>Core removes that Squarespace transaction fee and adds additional business features. Plus and Advanced provide more extensive commerce and selling capabilities.</p>

<p>Payment processing fees from the payment provider may still apply even when the Squarespace platform transaction fee is removed. Businesses should distinguish between these two types of charges when comparing plans.</p>

<ul style="padding-left: 24px;">

<p><b>Best for:</b> Small businesses that primarily sell physical or digital products online.</p>

<p><b>Starting paid price:</b> $29 per month on Basic with annual billing.</p>

<p><b>Free plan:</b> No permanent free plan; a trial is available.</p>

<p>Shopify is an ecommerce platform built to help businesses create and manage online stores. Unlike general website builders that include selling as one of several features, Shopify places product sales and store operations at the centre of its platform.</p>

<p>Businesses can create product pages, organise inventory, manage orders, configure checkout and sell through multiple sales channels. The platform also offers tools for shipping, payment processing and store management.</p>

<p>This makes Shopify particularly suitable for retailers, product brands and businesses that expect online sales to be a major part of their operations.</p>

<p>A business that only needs a few pages describing its services may not need Shopify's full ecommerce feature set. In that situation, a general website builder could offer better value.</p>

<h3 style="color: #1d4ed8; font-size: 21px; margin-top: 25px;">Key Features</h3>

<ul style="padding-left: 24px;">

<li>Product listings and inventory management.</li>
<li>Integrated checkout and payment options.</li>
<li>Order management and shipping tools.</li>
<li>Sales channels for online and in-person selling.</li>
<li>App marketplace for additional store functionality.</li>
<li>Analytics and reporting tools.</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Best for:</b> Businesses that publish articles, guides, resources and other content, or need a website that can be extended with plugins.</p>

<p><b>Starting paid price:</b> $4 per month on Personal with annual billing.</p>

<p><b>Free plan:</b> Yes.</p><p style="text-align: center;"><img alt="How To Use The WordPress Dashboard With Video &amp; Screenshots" class="sFlh5c FyHeAf uT7N3d" height="350" src="https://cdn.larryludwig.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/11/1-WP-Dashboard.png" style="--xgflle: 1.8298217179902756; max-width: 1129px;" width="640" />&nbsp;</p>

<p>WordPress.com is a hosted website platform built around the WordPress publishing system. It is particularly useful for businesses that expect to publish regular articles, maintain resource libraries or build a content-focused online presence.</p>

<p>Users can create pages and blog posts, choose themes, organise content and extend their websites with plugins on paid plans. This flexibility makes WordPress.com attractive to publishers, consultants, professional service providers and businesses that want more control over their content strategy.</p>

<p>It is important to distinguish WordPress.com from WordPress.org. WordPress.com provides managed hosting and platform services, while WordPress.org provides the open-source WordPress software that users can install on hosting they arrange separately.</p>

<p>With WordPress.com, hosting, security and many maintenance tasks are handled by the provider. This reduces the amount of technical administration required compared with managing a self-hosted WordPress installation.</p>

<h3 style="color: #1d4ed8; font-size: 21px; margin-top: 25px;">Key Features</h3>

<ul style="padding-left: 24px;">

<li>Customisable themes and website layouts.</li>
<li>Managed hosting and SSL security.</li>
<li>Plugin installation on paid plans.</li>
<li>SEO tools and content management capabilities.</li>
<li>Options for ecommerce through WooCommerce and other tools.</li>
<li>Website migration and developer features on eligible plans.</li>
</ul>

<ul style="padding-left: 24px;">
<li><b>Free:</b> $0, with a WordPress.com subdomain and 1GB storage.</li>
<li><b>Personal:</b> $4 per month.</li>
<li><b>Premium:</b> $8 per month.</li>
<li><b>Business:</b> $25 per month.</li>
<li><b>Commerce:</b> $45 per month.</li>

<p>The Personal plan includes a custom domain offer for the first year with eligible annual subscriptions, hosting and plugin installation. Premium adds features such as advanced design and marketing tools. Business provides additional resources and professional website capabilities, while Commerce is designed for businesses operating WooCommerce-powered online stores.</p>

<p>One important difference from some older WordPress.com comparisons is that plugin installation is now available on all paid plans, including Personal. Businesses should therefore compare the current plan restrictions rather than relying on outdated articles that claim plugins require only the Business plan.</p>

<p>The free plan is useful for experimenting with the platform, but it uses a WordPress.com subdomain and does not provide the full professional branding and plugin access available on paid plans.</p>

<p><b>Our verdict:</b> WordPress.com is worth considering for small businesses that want to publish content consistently and expand their website over time. Its managed hosting makes it easier to get started, while paid-plan plugin support provides room to add functionality as business requirements change.</p>

<p>It is particularly suitable for businesses that want a distinctive marketing website, a carefully designed portfolio or a content-driven site with custom layouts.</p>

<p>Unlike a basic drag-and-drop editor, Webflow provides more detailed design controls. Users can work with layout systems, reusable components and responsive settings to build websites that follow a specific visual identity.</p>

<p>This makes Webflow attractive to designers and businesses that have access to someone comfortable with website design. It may be less suitable for an owner who simply wants to publish a basic website as quickly as possible.</p>

<p>For a business publishing its own website, the relevant Site plan prices include:</p>

CMS is more appropriate for websites that publish collections of structured content, such as articles, resources or team profiles. Business offers higher limits and additional capabilities for larger websites.</p>

<p>Webflow also offers Workspace subscriptions for freelancers, agencies and teams. These have separate pricing and should be considered when multiple people need to design or manage websites.</p>

It is especially useful when a designer or technically confident team member will manage the website. <h3 style="color: #1d4ed8; font-size: 21px; margin-top: 25px;">Why Renewal Prices Matter</h3>

<p>For example, Hostinger has advertised a Premium package at $2.49 per month for a 48-month term, with $119.52 payable upfront and a listed renewal rate of $10.99 per month. The introductory rate and renewal rate are very different, even though both describe the same subscription.</p>

<p>Before purchasing, check the full amount due today, the length of the commitment, the renewal price and whether the domain offer expires after the first year. These details are particularly important for small businesses operating on tight budgets.</p>

<h2 style="border-bottom-color: rgb(219, 234, 254); border-bottom-style: solid; border-bottom-width: 2px; border-bottom: 2px solid rgb(219, 234, 254); color: #172554; font-size: 27px; margin-top: 42px; padding-bottom: 10px;">How to Choose the Right Website Builder</h2>

<p>Comparing website builders becomes easier when you start with your business requirements rather than the platform's feature list. Before paying for a subscription, consider the following factors.</p>

<h3 style="color: #1d4ed8; font-size: 21px; margin-top: 25px;">1. Decide What Your Website Needs to Do</h3>

<p>Start by defining the main purpose of the website. Is it meant to introduce your business, generate enquiries, display a portfolio, publish articles, accept bookings or sell products?</p>

<p>A simple service-business website may only need a homepage, services page, contact page and booking form. An online store will require product management, checkout, payment processing and inventory tools.</p>

<p>Choosing a platform based on these requirements helps avoid paying for features that your business does not need.</p>

<h3 style="color: #1d4ed8; font-size: 21px; margin-top: 25px;">2. Compare the Full Cost, Not Just the Advertised Price</h3>

<p>Look at the subscription's total cost over the first year and subsequent renewal periods. Check whether hosting, a custom domain, professional email and ecommerce functionality are included.</p>

<p>If you plan to sell online, calculate payment processing fees and any platform transaction fees. These charges can affect profitability, especially for businesses with small margins or a high volume of transactions.</p>

<h3 style="color: #1d4ed8; font-size: 21px; margin-top: 25px;">3. Check Mobile Responsiveness</h3>

<p>Customers may visit your website using smartphones, tablets and desktop computers. A professional website should display clearly across different screen sizes.</p>

<p>Check whether the builder offers mobile-responsive templates and whether you can preview and adjust the mobile version of your pages. Pay attention to navigation, image sizing, text readability and contact buttons.</p>

<h3 style="color: #1d4ed8; font-size: 21px; margin-top: 25px;">4. Examine SEO Features</h3>

<p>Search engine optimisation can help customers discover your business through search engines. A website builder should provide access to essential settings such as page titles, meta descriptions, image alternative text, custom URLs and mobile-friendly page layouts.</p>

<p>More advanced requirements may include redirects, structured data, sitemap management and greater control over technical settings.</p>

<p>Remember that having SEO tools does not guarantee search rankings. The quality of the content, website performance, competition and relevance to searchers also matter.</p>

<h3 style="color: #1d4ed8; font-size: 21px; margin-top: 25px;">5. Consider Future Growth and Portability</h3>

<p>Your business website may become more complex as the company grows. You might later need ecommerce, additional languages, a larger content library, customer accounts or advanced integrations.</p>

<p>Check whether the platform can support those requirements and whether you can export your content or move the website elsewhere if necessary.</p>

<p>Website portability differs considerably between providers. Some allow content exports but do not provide a complete export of the original design, functionality and platform-specific features. Understand these limitations before investing heavily in a platform.</p>

<h3 style="color: #1d4ed8; font-size: 21px; margin-top: 25px;">6. Test the Editor Before Committing</h3>

<p>Use a free plan or trial where available. Create a sample homepage, add a few images, edit the navigation and preview the mobile layout.</p>

<p>This gives you a better understanding of the platform's interface and learning curve. A builder may offer hundreds of features, but it is not necessarily the best choice if your team finds it difficult to use.</p>

<div style="border-radius: 7px; border: 1px solid rgb(226, 232, 240); margin: 18px 0px; padding: 18px;">
<h3 style="color: #1d4ed8; font-size: 18px; margin: 0px 0px 9px;">What is the best website builder for a small business?</h3>
<p style="margin: 0px;">The best option depends on the website's purpose. Wix is a strong all-around option, Squarespace suits design-focused service businesses, Hostinger is worth considering for budget-conscious websites, Shopify is designed for ecommerce, WordPress.com supports content-heavy websites, and Webflow provides greater visual design control.</p>
</div>

<div style="border-radius: 7px; border: 1px solid rgb(226, 232, 240); margin: 18px 0px; padding: 18px;">
<h3 style="color: #1d4ed8; font-size: 18px; margin: 0px 0px 9px;">Can I build a business website for free?</h3>
<p style="margin: 0px;">Yes. Platforms such as Wix and WordPress.com offer free plans, while Webflow provides a restricted free Starter option. However, free plans commonly have limitations involving custom domains, platform branding, storage, publishing features or ecommerce. A paid plan is usually more suitable for a professional business website.</p>
</div>

<div style="border-radius: 7px; border: 1px solid rgb(226, 232, 240); margin: 18px 0px; padding: 18px;">
<h3 style="color: #1d4ed8; font-size: 18px; margin: 0px 0px 9px;">How much does a small business website cost per month?</h3>
<p style="margin: 0px;">Website builder subscriptions can range from a few dollars per month to more than $100, depending on the platform, billing term and required features. Additional expenses may include domain renewal, professional email, premium apps, ecommerce fees and website design work.</p>
</div>

<div style="border-radius: 7px; border: 1px solid rgb(226, 232, 240); margin: 18px 0px; padding: 18px;">
<h3 style="color: #1d4ed8; font-size: 18px; margin: 0px 0px 9px;">Is Wix or Squarespace better for a small business?</h3>
<p style="margin: 0px;">Wix offers a broad range of business features and a free plan, making it a flexible choice for many small businesses. Squarespace is particularly attractive for businesses that prioritise polished design and professional presentation. Compare the features and total subscription cost against your specific needs before choosing.</p>

<h3 style="color: #1d4ed8; font-size: 18px; margin: 0px 0px 9px;">Which website builder is best for selling products online?</h3>
<p style="margin: 0px;">Shopify is a strong option for businesses whose main purpose is selling products online. It includes specialised tools for product listings, inventory, checkout and order management. Wix, Squarespace and WordPress.com also support ecommerce, but the best choice depends on store complexity and the features required.</p>

<h3 style="color: #1d4ed8; font-size: 18px; margin: 0px 0px 9px;">Do I need hosting if I use a website builder?</h3>
<p style="margin: 0px;">Most hosted website builders include hosting as part of their subscriptions. This means users generally do not need to purchase a separate hosting package. However, the arrangement differs when using self-hosted WordPress.org, where the website owner must arrange hosting independently.</p>

<div style="border-radius: 7px; border: 1px solid rgb(226, 232, 240); margin: 18px 0px; padding: 18px;">
<h3 style="color: #1d4ed8; font-size: 18px; margin: 0px 0px 9px;">Can I use my own domain name with a website builder?</h3>
<p style="margin: 0px;">Yes. Most paid website builder plans allow users to connect a custom domain, and some include a domain registration offer for the first year. Check whether the domain is included, how much it costs to renew and whether you can transfer it to another registrar later.</p>
</div>

<div style="border-radius: 7px; border: 1px solid rgb(226, 232, 240); margin: 18px 0px; padding: 18px;">
<h3 style="color: #1d4ed8; font-size: 18px; margin: 0px 0px 9px;">Is WordPress.com the same as WordPress.org?</h3>
<p style="margin: 0px;">No. WordPress.com is a hosted platform that provides website software, hosting and managed services. WordPress.org provides the open-source WordPress software, which users can install on hosting they arrange separately. The two options offer different levels of control and technical responsibility.</p>
</div>

<p>The best website builder is the one that supports your business goals without introducing unnecessary costs or complexity. A company that needs a simple online presence should not pay for advanced ecommerce features, while a retailer should not choose a basic platform that cannot support its sales operations.</p>

<p>Wix is a strong all-around option for businesses that want a flexible website with additional business tools. Squarespace is attractive for professional design, Hostinger offers budget-conscious entry options, and Shopify is designed for businesses that prioritise online sales.</p>

<p>WordPress.com is worth considering for content-driven businesses that want to expand their websites with plugins, while Webflow is a good fit for teams that need greater design control.</p>

<p>Before subscribing, compare the full cost of ownership, examine the renewal terms, test the editor and make sure the platform can support your future requirements.</p>
